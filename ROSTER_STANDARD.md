# Brokerage roster standard

A search result count is never proof of a complete company roster. No auto-verification or 80% acceptance threshold exists.

1. Broker selects the exact offices they manage. A franchise brand is not ownership evidence.
2. Read official directories and pagination with same-site, safe-URL restrictions. Record read, blocked, pending and rejected pages. Bounded time/page/item limits are reported, never hidden as success. Dynamic load-more results remain incomplete until a supported capture or alternate source resolves them.
3. Use profile URLs to deduplicate repeated cards; detect same-name ambiguity. Navigation/CTA text and staff biographies are not agent records. Default office assignments are suggestions and require review.
4. Compare unique names against the broker's independently supplied expected headcount and observed published totals. Any shortfall/excess, conflicting total or unread page remains unresolved. Never infer the expected headcount from names found. Model page-count claims cannot clear failed direct retrievals or be added together as disjoint reads.
5. Recover through another legitimate public source, an authorized supported MLS/feed connection, or roster CSV. Never bypass access controls. Public web data can vary by time/cache and does not prove current employment.
6. Save partial work as a draft. Broker confirmation requires an exact reconciled count, every agent assigned to a selected office, and explicit comparison against company records. Persist a server-issued attestation bound to the roster/count/office signature. Membership/office/count changes invalidate it; reorder and social-profile changes do not. Broker-confirmed is an attestation, not independent certification.
7. Advertising reports distinguish saved roster scope, agents searched and ad text assessed. A draft roster is prominently labeled; even confirmed rosters do not prove ad coverage or legal compliance.
